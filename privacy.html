Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Slicemail is a Google Sheets add-on that sends reports by email from data in the spreadsheet where it is installed. It is made and published by its independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: slicemail@enayatullah.com.

Who is responsible for your data

Your spreadsheet may hold other people's personal data, such as recipients' names and email addresses. For that data, you (or the organisation you work for) are the controller, and Google processes it under your agreement with Google. Slicemail runs inside your Google account and we have no access to it, so we are not your processor.

We are the controller only for the emails you send us at slicemail@enayatullah.com, and for the visit logs of this website described below.

What we can and cannot see

The add-on has no server. Nothing you open, read or send through it is copied to us or to any third party. It makes no analytics calls, sets no tracking identifiers, shows no advertising, and does not send error reports to us. We cannot see your spreadsheets, your Docs, your recipients or your emails.

What it reads

When you open it, and when a report you scheduled runs:

  • In the spreadsheet where it is installed: the cells, their displayed text, and their formatting (fill, text colour, bold, italic, alignment). The editor reads every tab so you can choose tables from it; previews and sends read the ranges your report names.
  • The Google Doc that holds your reports, including its tabs: the one Slicemail made, or one you chose in Google's Drive picker. It cannot open any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account's email address, to show your sender domain, to mark recipients outside it, and to name you in the log.

What it writes

Only when you ask it to:

  • Email sent from your own Google account to the recipients your report selects from your data. Mail is sent with Google's Apps Script mail service, so it counts against your daily sending quota.
  • A log tab called "Slicemail Log" in your spreadsheet, one row per send: time, report, result, reason, recipients, copied addresses, and a fingerprint of what was sent. It contains email addresses taken from your own data, and anyone who can view the spreadsheet can see it. You can delete the tab.
  • Tabs in your template Doc, when you create a report or save one from the editor.
  • Demo tabs in your spreadsheet, when you choose "Create demo data".
  • A Google Doc in your Drive, called "Slicemail reports" and the spreadsheet's name, when you choose "Get started". It holds your reports; you own it and can delete it.
  • A scheduled run, when you schedule a report: an hourly background run, under your account, builds the report when it is due and holds it. It sends nothing to your recipients until you approve it in the sidebar. It sends one email to you when a run is held, missed or failed; that email contains the spreadsheet's name and a link to it. Pausing or removing the schedule stops it at once; removing your last schedule removes the background run.

What it stores

In Google's Apps Script storage, attached to the spreadsheet, not with us:

  • Which Doc is your template, which tab is your roster, its email and tag columns, the sender name and reply-to address if you set them, how many recipients of each report were sent so far, each report's schedule with the email address of the person who set it, and a record of its last 14 scheduled runs (date, result, and a fingerprint of what was built).

These settings travel with the spreadsheet. Anyone who can edit the spreadsheet and runs Slicemail can see them, including the email address of whoever set a schedule.

In your browser, on your device: while you edit a report in the editor, a copy of the unsaved text, so it can be offered back if you close the window. It is removed when you save or discard it, or by clearing the browser's site data. It is not sent anywhere.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Removing the add-on or revoking its access (at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>) stops it and stops any scheduled runs. Your log tab, demo tabs, reports Doc and the settings stored in the spreadsheet stay until you delete them or the spreadsheet. We hold no copy of any of it.

What leaves your account

Only the emails you send, to the recipients you choose, through Google, and the alerts about your own scheduled runs, which go to you.

The add-on's sidebar and editor pages load two fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so Google receives a normal web request from your browser to serve them. No spreadsheet content is part of that request. When you choose a Doc in Settings, Google's Drive picker loads from apis.google.com and shows your own Drive to you; the add-on learns only the Doc you pick.

Google user data

Slicemail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data is used only to provide the add-on's visible features: previewing, scheduling and sending the reports you write. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties, not read by people, and not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

You may ask us for access to, correction, deletion or a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to restrict its use, or object to it, by writing to the address above. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
