It tells you what would go wrong first.

Before anything is sent, the preview lists what a careful person would catch. For the scorecard, it finds these two:

  • Lakeside's rows would go to no one. Nobody on the list manages Lakeside, so its sales would quietly drop out.
  • Hilltop's email would show #DIV/0! for Sunday's average order. Fix the formula in the sheet before it goes out.

Your data stays in your Google account.

No server.Slicemail runs inside Google Sheets as an add-on. Nothing passes through anyone else's computers.

Narrow access.It reads only the spreadsheet it is open in, and opens only the one Google Doc that holds your reports.

Sent from your Gmail.Emails go out from your own account, within your normal daily sending limit.

Nothing sends by itself.Scheduled reports are built for you and wait for your approval.
