Questions

Does anything send without my approval?

No. Emails go out when you press Send in the preview. A scheduled report is built for you on time, then waits for you to review and approve it.

Can I see every email before it sends?

Yes. The preview lists every email, who it goes to and how many rows it carries, and you can open each one as its recipient will see it.

What happens to rows with no recipient?

The preview says so before anything is sent, names how many rows would go to no one, and can select them in the sheet, so you can add the person or leave them out on purpose.

Does Slicemail upload my spreadsheet?

No. Slicemail is an add-on that runs as Google Apps Script inside your own Google account. There is no Slicemail server or database; your report never leaves Google.

Can each person get different rows?

Yes, that is what it does. Choose the column that says whose a row is, such as Site, Owner or Email, and each person's email carries only their rows.

Does it keep my sheet's formatting?

It keeps fills, text colours, bold, italic, alignment and number formats as the sheet shows them. Colours that come from conditional formatting are not carried over yet.

Does it work with Google Workspace?

Yes, with Workspace accounts where your admin allows add-ons, and with personal Google accounts. It sends through your account, so your normal daily sending limit applies.

What if someone leaves or changes role?

Recipients come from your sheet. Change the name or address there and the next preview follows it; rows left without anyone are flagged.
