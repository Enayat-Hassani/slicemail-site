Try the editor on demo data, with no install.

You keep one report as a tab in a Google Doc: the greeting, the text, and the tables you want from your Sheet. The add-on fills in the dates, picks the recipients from rules or from a roster in your data, and gives each group only its own rows.

Before anything is sent you see every email: who gets it, how many rows it carries, and the table as the recipient will see it. The preview flags what usually goes wrong, such as a site with data and no recipient, a formula error in a shown column, recipients outside your domain, an email too large for Gmail, and data that changed since you last looked. A send that no longer matches the preview is refused.

What it does:

  • Sends one email per site, team, client or person, from one template.
  • Takes recipients from your roster tab or from the data itself.
  • Keeps your table formatting: fills, text colours, bold, italic, alignment.
  • Resolves dates such as last week, this month or the same period last year.
  • Includes a block editor with pickers, a live preview, and a "preview as of another day".
  • Offers a test send to yourself, and a run log tab in your own spreadsheet.

What it does not do: it has no server and collects no analytics, so the publisher never sees your data. Your data stays in your Google account, and mail goes out only when you confirm.

It runs inside one spreadsheet. A report can run on a schedule (daily, weekdays, weekly or monthly, within the hour you choose): each run is built and held, you get an email, and nothing goes to your recipients until you approve it in the sidebar.
